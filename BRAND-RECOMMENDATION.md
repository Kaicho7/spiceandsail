# Brand Canonical Recommendation — Spice and Sail Zanzibar

## Executive Summary
**Canonical brand: "Spice and Sail Zanzibar" (domain: spiceandsail.com)**

Rationale:
- **Search differentiation**: "Spice and Sail" alone conflicts with Greek brand `sailandspice.gr` (sailing tours) and generic "spice and sail" queries. Adding "Zanzibar" creates a unique, geo-qualified entity that ranks for "Zanzibar tours", "Zanzibar hotels", "Zanzibar transfers".
- **Historical clarity**: Original name "Dho Coast Travels" has zero search volume and no brand equity. Retire it entirely — no redirects, no mentions.
- **Legal registration**: Company is registered with Zanzibar Commission for Tourism (ZCT) as a Zanzibar-based tour operator. The brand must match the registered entity for licence display compliance.
- **Domain authority**: `spiceandsail.com` is the established domain (3+ years). All canonical signals (hreflang, sitemap, schema, GSC) point here.

## Implementation
1. **Site-wide**: Replace all "Dho Coast Travels" references with "Spice and Sail Zanzibar" (already done in this sprint).
2. **Schema.org Organization**: `@type: "TravelAgency"`, `name: "Spice and Sail Zanzibar"`, `alternateName: ["Spice and Sail", "Spice & Sail Zanzibar"]`, `url: "https://spiceandsail.com"`, `address: {addressCountry: "TZ", addressRegion: "Zanzibar"}`.
3. **Google Business Profile**: Claim/verify as "Spice and Sail Zanzibar" with ZCT licence number when available.
4. **Social handles**: @SpiceAndSailZanzibar (Instagram, Facebook, X) — consistent NAP.
5. **Internal links**: All breadcrumbs, footer, schema use "Spice and Sail Zanzibar".
6. **Meta titles**: Format `"{Page Title} — Spice and Sail Zanzibar"` (already implemented).

## Do Not Use
- "Spice & Sail" (ampersand) — inconsistent, confuses search.
- "Spice and Sail" without "Zanzibar" — ambiguous.
- "Dho Coast Travels" — zero equity, legal liability if used without registration.

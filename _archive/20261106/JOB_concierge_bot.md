## Goal
A functional, honest, zero-cost concierge for spiceandsail.com:
- Works with NO paid service. The core is deterministic code. A free-tier LLM is an optional layer that may be missing, rate-limited or removed at any time, and the bot must then keep working in guided mode.
- Every enquiry is saved to persistent storage BEFORE the visitor sees success, with a server-issued reference.
- Nothing is ever called "locked", "confirmed", "instant checkout" or "24/7". Availability is always "pending team confirmation".
